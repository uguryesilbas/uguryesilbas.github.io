VocaNectar Türkçe

Vocabulary flashcards in 8 languages

Build your vocabulary by flipping cards — no account, no server.

Choose your own language and the one you want to learn from Turkish, English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese and Russian.

VocaNectar keeps the words you learn, your progress and your own custom words on your device and in your own iCloud account. It requires no user account and never sends your data to a server of ours; the developer cannot access it.
